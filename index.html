    Executive summaryRingkasan eksekutif

    Since 2025 an offer letter needs at least RM15,000 in a TH account. In September 2026 Tabung Haji went further with Seruan Istito'ah, its Hajj Preparation Plan: from 1 January 2029 depositors join the Hajj queue automatically once they hold RM15,000, and those already registered have until 31 December 2028 to get there. To act on that, TH needs to see readiness across 9.7 million depositors, not one account at a time.Sejak 2025 surat tawaran memerlukan sekurang-kurangnya RM15,000 dalam akaun TH. Pada September 2026 Tabung Haji melangkah lebih jauh dengan Seruan Istito'ah, Pelan Persediaan Haji: mulai 1 Januari 2029 pendeposit layak giliran haji secara automatik apabila memiliki RM15,000, dan yang sudah berdaftar diberi tempoh sehingga 31 Disember 2028. Untuk bertindak, TH perlu melihat kesediaan 9.7 juta pendeposit, bukan satu akaun pada satu masa.

    SEFB studies how people save, why they stop, and which nudges keep them going. This proposal joins that behavioural research to a production-grade visualisation platform: one dashboard where TH leadership can see, in seconds, how many depositors have reached RM15,000, their own Bayaran Haji and the full Kos Haji of RM33,300, by state, age group and corporate category.SEFB mengkaji cara orang menabung, sebab mereka berhenti dan dorongan yang mengekalkan mereka. Cadangan ini menggabungkan penyelidikan tingkah laku tersebut dengan platform visualisasi bertaraf pengeluaran: satu papan pemuka di mana kepimpinan TH boleh melihat dalam beberapa saat berapa ramai pendeposit telah mencapai RM15,000, Bayaran Haji sendiri dan Kos Haji penuh RM33,300, mengikut negeri, kumpulan umur dan kategori korporat.

    The same dashboard measures literacy work. Each SEFB module sends anonymous events into it, so TH sees which programmes move depositors up the ladder, not only how many people attended.Papan pemuka yang sama mengukur kerja literasi. Setiap modul SEFB menghantar acara tanpa nama ke dalamnya, supaya TH melihat program mana yang benar-benar menggerakkan pendeposit, bukan sekadar bilangan kehadiran.

    This site is the proof-of-concept. Every page works. The figures are synthetic until Phase 1 connects pseudonymised data inside TH.Laman ini ialah bukti konsep. Setiap halaman berfungsi. Angkanya sintetik sehingga Fasa 1 menyambungkan data tanpa nama di dalam TH.

    TH knows what each depositor holds. It cannot yet see, at a glance, who is on track for Hajj, who has stalled, and which programme would help.TH tahu baki setiap pendeposit. Namun TH belum dapat melihat dengan sekali pandang siapa yang di landasan untuk haji, siapa yang terhenti, dan program mana yang boleh membantu.

    Readiness data sits across systems, branches and campaigns. Literacy programmes report attendance, not changes in saving. Leaders receive reports after the quarter ends, not a view they can question in the meeting.Data kesediaan tersebar di pelbagai sistem, cawangan dan kempen.
