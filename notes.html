What this proposal rests on, what is made up for the demonstration, and what TH needs to confirm before any figure is used for a decision.Asas cadangan ini, apa yang direka untuk demonstrasi, dan apa yang perlu disahkan TH sebelum sebarang angka digunakan untuk keputusan.

What is officialApa yang rasmi

  • Kos Haji of RM33,300 per Muassasah pilgrim for 1448H/2027M, unchanged since 1445H/2024M; Bayaran Haji of RM15,000 (B40), RM23,500 (M40) and RM33,300 (T20 and successful appeals); HAFIS of RM17,300 plus RM1,000 from the Government for B40 and RM9,800 for M40.Kos Haji RM33,300 bagi seorang jemaah Muassasah untuk 1448H/2027M, kekal sejak 1445H/2024M; Bayaran Haji RM15,000 (B40), RM23,500 (M40) dan RM33,300 (T20 dan rayuan berjaya); HAFIS RM17,300 serta RM1,000 daripada Kerajaan bagi B40 dan RM9,800 bagi M40.
  • Offer letters need at least RM15,000 in the TH account; 31,600 places for 1448H/2027M; offer letters from 21 September 2026.Surat tawaran memerlukan sekurang-kurangnya RM15,000 dalam akaun TH; 31,600 kuota bagi 1448H/2027M; surat tawaran mula dikeluarkan 21 September 2026.
  • Seruan Istito'ah: automatic queue eligibility at RM15,000 from 1 January 2029; registered depositors have until 31 December 2028; Tabung Istito'ah beside Tabung Am.Seruan Istito'ah: kelayakan giliran automatik pada RM15,000 mulai 1 Januari 2029; pendeposit berdaftar diberi tempoh sehingga 31 Disember 2028; Tabung Istito'ah di samping Tabung Am.
  • 9.7 million depositors and a 3.50% profit distribution for 2025 (3.25% for 2024).9.7 juta pendeposit dan agihan keuntungan 3.50% bagi 2025 (3.25% bagi 2024).
  • The 13 states and 3 federal territories, and the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709).13 negeri dan 3 wilayah persekutuan, serta Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010 (Akta 709).

What is syntheticApa yang sintetik

  • Every depositor figure on the tracker: a seeded model of balances by state, age group and category. Only its total is scaled to TH's 9.7 million. It must not be read as describing any real state.Setiap angka pendeposit pada penjejak: model baki bertitik mula mengikut negeri, kumpulan umur dan kategori. Hanya jumlahnya diskala kepada 9.7 juta pendeposit TH. Ia tidak boleh dibaca sebagai gambaran mana-mana negeri sebenar.
  • The share of depositors registered for Hajj and the 2028 watchlist counts.Peratus pendeposit yang berdaftar haji dan bilangan dalam senarai pantau 2028.
  • The four depositors on the simulator page.Empat pendeposit di halaman simulasi.
  • The example targets (40%, 25%, 17% and 12%) and the programme funnel.Contoh sasaran (40%, 25%, 17% dan 12%) dan corong program.

    For TH to confirmUntuk disahkan oleh TH

    1. Payment category before the offer. The category is fixed on the offer letter. How should the tracker project B40, M40 or T20 earlier, and should repeat pilgrims (no HAFIS) be shown apart?Kategori bayaran sebelum tawaran. Kategori ditetapkan pada surat tawaran. Bagaimana penjejak patut mengunjurkan B40, M40 atau T20 lebih awal, dan adakah jemaah berulang (tanpa HAFIS) perlu dipaparkan berasingan?

    2. Which balance counts. For the RM15,000 gate, does the tracker read Tabung Istito'ah only, or Tabung Am as well?Baki yang dikira. Bagi ambang RM15,000, adakah penjejak membaca Tabung Istito'ah sahaja, atau Tabung Am juga?

    3. Registration and queue data. Registration date and expected offer year per registrant, so readiness can be read against how soon each turn comes.Data pendaftaran dan giliran. Tarikh daftar dan jangkaan tahun tawaran bagi setiap pendaftar, supaya kesediaan dapat dibaca berbanding bila giliran tiba.

    4. Cost scenarios. Which Kos Haji and assistance scenarios leadership wants to see beyond the +10% and +20% in this prototype.Senario kos. Senario Kos Haji dan bantuan yang ingin dilihat oleh kepimpinan selain +10% dan +20% dalam prototaip ini.

    5. Segments and categories. Age bands (18–29, 30–44, 45–59, 60+) and the four corporate categories are proposals for the Phase 1 data mapping.Segmen dan kategori. Jalur umur (18–29, 30–44, 45–59, 60+) dan empat kategori korporat ialah cadangan untuk pemetaan data Fasa 1.

    6. Refresh, hosting and access. Quarterly refresh assumed; monthly is possible. Hosting inside TH and the role list are to be agreed.Kekerapan, pengehosan dan akses. Kemas kini suku tahunan diandaikan; bulanan boleh dibuat. Pengehosan di dalam TH dan senarai peranan akan dipersetujui.

    7. Budget, team and start date. To be confirmed with SEFB, RMC and TH.Bajet, pasukan dan tarikh mula. Akan disahkan bersama SEFB, RMC dan TH.

    Terms usedIstilah

    Istito'ah
    Being able to perform Hajj: in money, knowledge, health and safe passage.Kemampuan menunaikan haji: dari segi kewangan, ilmu, kesihatan dan keselamatan perjalanan.
    Seruan Istito'ah
    TH's Hajj Preparation Plan announced in September 2026: automatic queue eligibility at RM15,000 from 2029, and Tabung Istito'ah.Pelan Persediaan Haji TH yang diumumkan pada September 2026: kelayakan giliran automatik pada RM15,000 mulai 2029, dan Tabung Istito'ah.
    Kos Haji
    The full cost of Hajj per Muassasah pilgrim: RM33,300 for 1448H/2027M.Kos penuh haji bagi seorang jemaah Muassasah: RM33,300 bagi 1448H/2027M.
    Bayaran Haji
    What the pilgrim pays after any assistance; set by category.Amaun yang dibayar jemaah selepas bantuan; ditetapkan mengikut kategori.
    HAFIS
    Bantuan Kewangan Haji: TH's targeted Hajj assistance for first-time B40 and M40 Muassasah pilgrims.Bantuan Kewangan Haji: bantuan bersasar TH bagi jemaah Muassasah B40 dan M40 kali pertama.
    Muassasah
    Pilgrims whose Hajj is managed by TH, as opposed to licensed operators (PJH).Jemaah yang urusan hajinya dikendalikan oleh TH, berbanding pengelola berlesen (PJH).
    Profit distributionAgihan keuntungan
    What TH declares to depositors each year after audit; not a fixed or promised return.Agihan yang diisytiharkan TH kepada pendeposit setiap tahun selepas audit; bukan pulangan tetap atau dijanjikan.
    PseudonymisationPenyahnamaan
    Replacing identifiers with a code that cannot be reversed outside TH.Menggantikan pengenal dengan kod yang tidak boleh diterbalikkan di luar TH.
    PoC
    Proof-of-concept: a working version that shows the idea holds before full rollout.Bukti konsep: versi berfungsi yang menunjukkan idea itu boleh dilaksanakan sebelum pelaksanaan penuh.
