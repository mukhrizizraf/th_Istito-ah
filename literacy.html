Literacy that shows up in the numbersLiterasi yang terlihat dalam angka

SEFB colleagues build the modules. Each one sends a small set of anonymous events into the tracker, so TH sees what changed in people's saving, not only who attended.Rakan SEFB membina modul. Setiap modul menghantar sebilangan kecil acara tanpa nama ke penjejak, supaya TH melihat perubahan dalam simpanan, bukan sekadar siapa yang hadir.

Four layers, one ladderEmpat lapisan, satu tangga

Each layer moves depositors one step and is measured by one figure on the dashboard.Setiap lapisan menggerakkan pendeposit satu langkah dan diukur oleh satu angka pada papan pemuka.

1

AwarenessKesedaran

Seruan Istito'ah messages through THiJARI, TH branches, mosques and employers, starting with the RM15,000 gate and the 2028 deadline.Mesej Seruan Istito'ah melalui THiJARI, cawangan TH, masjid dan majikan, bermula dengan ambang RM15,000 dan tarikh akhir 2028.

Measured by: module enrolmentDiukur dengan: pendaftaran modul
2

PlanningPerancangan

TH's Kalkulator Haji and SEFB savings blueprints fix a target year and a monthly amount for RM15,000 and the own payment.Kalkulator Haji TH dan pelan simpanan SEFB menetapkan tahun sasaran dan jumlah bulanan bagi RM15,000 dan bayaran sendiri.

Measured by: plans createdDiukur dengan: pelan dibuat
3

HabitTabiat

Direct debit or salary deduction into Tabung Istito'ah, so saving no longer depends on memory.Debit terus atau potongan gaji ke Tabung Istito'ah, supaya menabung tidak lagi bergantung pada ingatan.

Measured by: regular saversDiukur dengan: penyimpan tetap
4

ReadinessKesediaan

A pre-departure check across money, knowledge, health and family support, before the offer letter arrives.Semakan pra-pemergian merangkumi kewangan, ilmu, kesihatan dan sokongan keluarga, sebelum surat tawaran tiba.

Measured by: own payment covered, fewer deferralsDiukur dengan: bayaran sendiri dicapai, kurang penangguhan

Trace a module to the dashboardJejak modul ke papan pemuka

Pick a module. The pipeline and the event table show exactly what it sends and which tiles it feeds.Pilih modul. Saluran dan jadual acara menunjukkan apa yang dihantar dan jubin yang disalurkan.

Telemetry eventsAcara telemetri

Highlighted rows are sent by the chosen module. No event carries a name, IC or account number.Baris berwarna dihantar oleh modul yang dipilih. Tiada acara membawa nama, nombor KP atau nombor akaun.

What the programme funnel looks likeRupa corong program

Synthetic. In the real tool every step is counted from events, by state and segment.Sintetik. Dalam alat sebenar setiap langkah dikira daripada acara, mengikut negeri dan segmen.

How we will know a module worksCara kami tahu modul berkesan

  • A short literacy quiz before and after each module.Kuiz literasi ringkas sebelum dan selepas setiap modul.
  • Enrolled depositors compared with similar depositors who did not enrol, on deposit regularity and time to RM15,000.Pendeposit yang mendaftar dibandingkan dengan pendeposit serupa yang tidak mendaftar, dari segi kekerapan simpanan dan masa ke RM15,000.
  • The deferral rate among offerees, now reported at 18%, tracked by module and segment.Kadar penangguhan dalam kalangan penerima tawaran, kini dilaporkan 18%, dijejak mengikut modul dan segmen.
  • Results reported each quarter on the dashboard and in the Phase 3 evaluation report.Keputusan dilaporkan setiap suku tahun di papan pemuka dan dalam laporan penilaian Fasa 3.
