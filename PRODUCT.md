# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, multi-page, no build step, deployable to GitHub Pages (same pattern as the owner's kedah-silver-economy site). Confirmed by the owner.

## Users

- **Primary:** Lembaga Tabung Haji (TH) executive leadership and strategy/Hajj-readiness teams in Kuala Lumpur, evaluating a collaboration proposal from UUM after the Vice-Chancellor and Research Management Centre (RMC) courtesy visit. They read it on a laptop or projector in a meeting, and on a phone afterwards.
- **Secondary:** UUM RMC and SEFB colleagues who co-own the proposal and present it.

## Product Purpose

An interactive proposal: it argues for an SEFB–TH research and development collaboration and demonstrates the core deliverable, the "Tabung Haji Istito'ah Readiness & Literacy Tracker", as a working proof-of-concept. Success means TH leadership can see, filter and question depositor readiness the way the real tool would let them, and agrees to the three-phase engagement.

## Positioning

Merges SEFB's behavioural financial-literacy research with a production-style dashboard. The literacy modules are designed to stream telemetry into the same dashboard, so programme effort and depositor readiness are measured in one place, aligned with TH's own policy: the RM15,000 minimum for offer letters (2025) and the Seruan Istito'ah Hajj Preparation Plan announced in September 2026 (automatic queue eligibility at RM15,000 from 1 January 2029; registered depositors have until 31 December 2028; Tabung Istito'ah). The brief dated Seruan Istito'ah to 2025; TH's own material dates the plan to September 2026.

## Operating Context

- Proposal structure is fixed by the brief (`../th_dashboard_proposal_prompt (1).docx`): 1 Executive Summary; 2 Methodology by web-development phases (Phase 1 Data Architecture & Asset Mapping; Phase 2 User-Centric Design & KPI Workshops with 25% / 50% / 100% package thresholds; Phase 3 Interactive PoC Dashboard Deployment); 3 Technical Deliverable Blueprint (layout schema, state / corporate-category filters, modular gauges, ecosystem connectivity map); 4 Aligned Literacy Programme Layers (modules streaming telemetry).
- The owner is the **Technical App Development Lead**; the proposal must make that role explicit (shown in the overview facts strip and the open Team and roles section).
- Info notes and an optional Presenter-notes mode exist so the owner can pitch without mis-explaining a panel.
- Tone: advanced, technically precise, authoritative academic and engineering posture, consistent with Shariah-compliant corporate guidelines.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual: English with a Bahasa Melayu toggle on every page. Confirmed.
- All dashboard figures are synthetic and must be labelled as such; no real TH depositor data is available.
- Undecided (shown as "To be confirmed"): budget, other team members, exact start date. Timeline is a proposed 12 months, marked "for discussion".
- TH's published 1448H/2027M figures are used as facts with sources (Kos Haji RM33,300; Bayaran Haji B40 RM15,000, M40 RM23,500, T20 and appeals RM33,300; HAFIS; the RM15,000 gate; 31,600 places; 9.7 million depositors; profit distribution 3.50% for 2025). Future cost changes and future profit distributions are scenarios, never forecasts or promises.
- The readiness ladder runs on TH's amounts (RM8,325 / RM15,000 / RM23,500 / RM33,300) instead of the brief's 25/50/100%; Phase 2 confirms it with TH. Decided 4 Oct 2026 after reading TH's figures.
- Personal data handling must reference PDPA 2010 and aggregate-only views.

## Brand Commitments

- Sister site to the owner's Kedah Silver Economy dashboard (https://mukhrizizraf.github.io/kedah-silver-economy_claude-version/): same multi-page pitch structure, shell, pager and light/dark behaviour.
- No blue anywhere (owner's standing preference recorded in the Kedah site).
- Co-branded lockup: SEFB (UUM) logo beside the Lembaga Tabung Haji logo, on a light plate, at the owner's request (4 Oct 2026). The site remains clearly a UUM proposal: the footer states it is not an official TH product.

## Evidence on Hand

- The brief (docx above) and TH's public 1448H/2027M documents (FAQ PDF, cost page, Seruan Istito'ah page, 18 March 2026 press release), cited in the site.
- No real depositor data, no confirmed budget, no named co-researchers, no testimonials. Do not fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't claim: every promise in the proposal has a working, clickable counterpart.
2. Honest by default: synthetic data and assumptions are labelled where they appear.
3. Leadership first: answers "how ready are our depositors, where, and what moves them" within one screen.
4. Research to interface: each literacy module traces to a metric on the dashboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable on projector and phone; colour never carries meaning alone (labels, icons, table views); keyboard navigable; respects reduced motion.
